Vēlinai

Population: ~35 515 332

Size: ~467 307 km2

Location: A tri-island elven realm in the southern-center of Frexis, west of a vast abandoned landmass known only as the Island of Death.

Description: Vēlinai is a densely populated elven kingdom forged by migration, discipline, and lineage. Following a great influx of peoples from lost eastern settlements, the realm transformed into a highly ordered state where survival demanded planning, unity, and restraint. Society is structured around five ancient bloodlines, each entrusted with specific duties within the realm. Though ruled by a single crown, power in Vēlinai is carefully balanced, and much of its deeper history is deliberately obscured.

Climate: Cold temperate with mild southern influences. Temperatures range roughly from -4 °C in winter to 15 °C in summer. Coastal regions are damp and cool, while inland forests and highlands experience longer winters and heavy snowfall.

Economy: A centrally managed, highly regulated economy shaped by the need to sustain a vast population on limited land. Agriculture is intensive and efficient, supported by forestry, fisheries, and internal trade. Guilds and estates are tightly overseen, prioritizing stability and long-term planning over excess wealth.

Military: A large, disciplined defensive force dominated by the Geltvarēnai Bloodline. Emphasis is placed on heavy infantry, fortified settlements, and coastal protection. Naval forces maintain constant patrols between the islands and guard eastern waters, while small expeditionary forces maintain distant coastal outposts.

Culture: Vēlinai culture values order, ancestry, and restraint. Bloodlines define social roles, and lineage is regarded as both duty and destiny. Art favors symmetry, pale stone, dark wood, and muted colors. History is respected but selectively taught, and silence is often seen as a virtue rather than absence.

Government: Monarchy. The Auksarēnai Bloodline holds the crown, while the remaining four bloodlines rule as archducal and ducal houses, each governing territories and institutions according to inherited roles.

Exports: Timber and refined wood goods, preserved fish, stonework, precision-crafted tools, arms, and trained administrators.
